About Us

Custody with purpose.

The Department of Corrections is responsible for the custody, supervision, and rehabilitation of individuals who have been convicted of crimes. Its primary goal is not only to enforce lawful sentences but also to provide inmates with the tools and support needed to change their behavior.

Through structured programs such as education, job training, and counseling, the Department works to address the underlying causes of criminal activity. By focusing on personal development and accountability, it aims to prepare inmates for successful reintegration into society, reducing repeat offenses and promoting safer communities.
